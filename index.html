Marcelo Oliveira Ribeiro

Ph.D. in Mathematics
Assistant Professor, Federal University of Maranhão, Brazil

My research lies in transcendental number theory — the study of numbers, such as π and e, that satisfy no polynomial equation with rational coefficients. I work mostly at the intersection of Diophantine approximation, Schanuel's Conjecture, and questions of irrationality. For more information please see the following:
