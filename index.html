Marcelo Oliveira Ribeiro

PhD in Mathematics
Assistant Professor
Federal University of Maranhão – UFMA, Brazil

My research interests lie in transcendental number theory. At heart, I am drawn to numbers that slip past every algebraic equation meant to capture them, such as π and e. My work sits at the intersection of Diophantine approximation, Schanuel's Conjecture, and questions of irrationality.
