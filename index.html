About

Marcelo works in transcendental number theory — the study of numbers that slip past every algebraic equation meant to capture them, such as π and e. His research sits at the intersection of Diophantine approximation, Schanuel's Conjecture, and open questions of irrationality.

He is Brazilian, and teaches and writes about mathematics from Maranhão, in Brazil's northeast.
